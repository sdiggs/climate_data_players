About

Round nodes are organizations active in stewardship. Dark squares are federal producers. Faded dots are partner-tier contributors.

What this shows (v2.0). ~100 organizations across the global response, including coalitions, repositories, international governance bodies, European archives, live observational networks, and funders. Solid ties are explicit coordination relationships. Dashed ties point to federal producers whose data an org works with. Dotted ties are operates/parent-of relationships. Green ties are funding flows.

Status discipline. Per the handoff: A/B/C/D status describes evidence of crisis-response activity, not operational importance. B orgs are major infrastructure (NOAA NCEI, NCAR, DKRZ, Argo, GO-SHIP), but their current rescue role is not independently documented. Default view: A+B.

Peer-initiative frame. KCF DCI anchors the producer end — sustaining the observations themselves. NYCE DSI anchors the access end — governance, usability, public-facing tools.
